Org Assistant connects Emacs and Google Drive to help its owner manage plain-text Org-mode notes, tasks, and agendas.

Made for personal use

This is a private workflow for its owner, rather than a public service. There are no public sign-ups or accounts on this website.

The Google Drive integration reads and writes Org files using a dedicated Google account. The owner chooses which personal folders to make available by sharing them with that account through Google Drive.

Changes are currently made through approved, manual operations, with conflict checks and versioned backups. Automatic synchronization and autonomous AI editing are disabled.
